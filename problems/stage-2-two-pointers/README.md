# Stage 2 · Two pointers

**Goal:** solve problems on sequences by walking two positions through them, often from both ends toward the middle, instead of comparing everything with everything.

The full primer gets written when you reach this stage. It will introduce the **loop invariant** (a statement that stays true before and after every pass of a loop, and explains why the loop is correct). That idea comes back in binary search and trees.
